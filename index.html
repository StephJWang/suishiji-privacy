岁时纪：今昔千年历 隐私政策

本隐私政策适用于 Android 应用“岁时纪：今昔千年历”（繁体名称“歲時紀：今昔千年曆”，下称“本应用”），以及本隐私政策网页。本应用由王君（Jun Wang）开发，以 Jun Wang Studio 名称发布。下文“我们”指该开发者。

岁时纪提供公历与农历日期浏览、历史上的今天、名人生卒、传统节日与节气、历史纪年以及古画欣赏等功能。历史人物的姓名、生卒日期和历史事件属于应用提供的内容，不是要求您提交的个人资料。

阅读摘要

核心日期查询和阅读在设备上完成；无需注册应用账号。我们不运营收集阅读记录的后台，不在应用中投放广告，也不加入用于行为分析或广告画像的跟踪工具。系统备份、Google Play 服务、您主动发送的邮件和本政策网页的托管服务，可能涉及下文说明的独立数据处理。

1. 适用范围与当前版本

本政策说明本应用当前提供的功能如何处理信息，并区分应用本地处理、开发者实际收到的信息，以及 Google、GitHub 等服务提供商的处理。Google Play 账号不等于岁时纪应用账号；本应用目前不提供账号注册、云端个人资料或开发者运营的云同步服务。

截至本政策更新日期，当前内测版本尚未开放真实的应用内购买。下文会说明 Google Play 分发服务及已包含的结算组件，但不会把尚未开放的交易描述为已发生的数据收集。

2. 在设备上处理的信息

日期与界面状态：应用使用设备提供的本地日期和时间显示“今天”，并在您操作时处理选中的日期、农历或公历模式、日历或月历页面等状态。这些信息用于显示和计算，不会作为您的查询历史或阅读记录发送给开发者。应用不读取系统日历中的私人日程，也不会要求您输入本人的生日。

文字偏好：您选择的简体或繁体显示方式保存在应用的设备本地设置中，以便下次启动时恢复。该设置不包含您的姓名、邮箱或其他账号资料。

内容与缓存：历史文字、历法数据、字体和古画由安装包及其资源包提供。加载和显示这些内容时，应用可在设备上使用临时内存或系统管理的缓存。核心阅读不需要把所浏览的日期、文字或画作上传给开发者。

3. Android 备份与设备迁移

本应用目前允许 Android 系统备份。根据您的设备、系统版本、备份服务提供商及个人设置，系统可能将应用本地设置等符合备份条件的数据备份到您的备份账户，或在更换设备时迁移这些数据。使用 Google 备份的设备通常由 Google 提供该服务。

系统备份不等于开发者收到这些数据：我们无法通过自己的服务器读取您的系统备份。您可以在设备的系统设置及备份服务中管理备份、恢复和删除。卸载应用或清除本地数据，不一定同时删除已经存在的云端备份。

4. 权限、广告与跟踪

本应用当前不申请访问定位、通讯录、短信、通话记录、相机、麦克风或个人相册的权限，也不需要读取您的私人日程。当前应用不投放广告，不集成广告 SDK、用户行为分析 SDK 或另行添加的崩溃上报 SDK，不出售个人资料，也不建立广告用户画像。Google Play 平台自身的技术报告见下一节。

安装包包含 Google Play 结算及其相关组件所声明的网络和网络状态权限。具有网络权限并不表示开发者会收到您的阅读记录。Google Play 自身的分发、安全和系统服务所进行的处理，见下一节。

5. Google Play、资源分发与购买功能

当您通过 Google Play 下载、安装、更新本应用及古画资源包，或使用其许可和安装安全检查时，Google 可能根据您使用的服务处理 Google 账号、应用版本、设备及安装相关信息。这些处理用于分发、兼容性、安全和相关平台功能，受 Google 的隐私政策及您的 Google 设置约束；不应将其理解为开发者获得了这些平台掌握的全部资料。

在您允许设备共享使用情况和诊断数据等适用条件下，Google Play 还可能通过 Android vitals 等平台功能向开发者提供汇总统计和技术诊断信息，例如应用版本、设备型号、Android 版本、崩溃及应用无响应报告。我们仅将可获得的相关报告用于排查问题、改善稳定性和兼容性，不用来建立广告画像。这与应用把您浏览的具体日期或历史条目上传给我们不同。相关共享设置由 Android 和 Google 服务管理。

应用已包含 Google Play Billing 结算组件，但当前内测版本没有启用真实购买流程。如果界面显示“暂未开放购买”，该入口不会发起真实交易；应用当前也不会通过该流程向开发者发送订单或购买凭证。我们不会通过本应用索取您的银行卡号、支付密码或完整支付卡资料。

在启用真实购买、购买恢复或凭证验证前，我们会根据实际实现更新本政策，说明购买状态、商品标识或购买凭证等信息的处理方式。Google Play 已经持有的账户、交易或下载记录，仍由 Google 按其规则管理。

Suishiji Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app 岁时纪：今昔千年历, also named 歲時紀：今昔千年曆 (referred to as “Suishiji” or the “App”), and to this privacy-policy website. The App is developed by Jun Wang (王君) and published under the name Jun Wang Studio. “We”, “us” and “our” refer to the developer.

Suishiji offers Gregorian and Chinese lunar calendar browsing, historical events on this day, notable historical births and deaths, traditional festivals and solar terms, historical eras, and classical Chinese paintings. The historical names, life dates and events included in its content are not personal information that you are asked to submit.

We do not operate a backend that collects your reading history, display in-app advertising, or add tracking tools for behavioral analytics or advertising profiles. 1. Scope and current release

This policy describes the App's current features and distinguishes on-device processing, information actually received by the developer, and processing by service providers such as Google and GitHub. A Google Play account is not a Suishiji account. The App currently has no account registration, cloud-based personal profile, or developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

As of the last-updated date of this policy, real in-app purchases are not enabled in the current internal-testing release. We describe Google Play distribution and the included billing component below without presenting transactions that are not yet enabled as existing data collection.

2. Information processed on your device

Dates and interface state. The App uses the local date and time supplied by your device to display “today”. It also processes your selected date, lunar or Gregorian mode, and daily or monthly view while you use it. These values support display and calculations; they are not sent to the developer as search or reading history. The App does not read private events from your system calendar or ask for your own birthday.

Text preferences. Your choice of Simplified or Traditional Chinese is stored in the App's local settings so it can be restored the next time you open the App. This setting does not contain your name, email address or other account information.

Content and caches. Historical text, calendar data, fonts and paintings are provided through the App and its asset packs. Loading and displaying this content may use temporary memory or system-managed caches on your device. Core reading does not require uploading the dates, text or paintings you view to the developer.

3. Android backups and device transfers

The App currently permits Android system backups. Depending on your device, operating-system version, backup provider and personal settings, the system may back up eligible App data, such as local settings, to your backup account, or transfer that data when you change devices. On devices using Google backup, this service is generally provided by Google.

A system backup does not mean that the developer receives the data. We cannot read your system backups through our own server. You can manage backup, restoration and deletion through your device settings and backup service. Uninstalling the App or clearing its local data does not necessarily delete existing cloud backups.

4. Permissions, advertising and tracking

The current App does not request access to your location, contacts, SMS, call history, camera, microphone or personal photo library, and does not need access to your private calendar events. It does not display advertising or integrate advertising SDKs, behavioral-analytics SDKs, or an additional crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal information or create advertising profiles. Technical reports provided by the Google Play platform itself are described in the next section.

The installation package includes internet and network-state permissions declared by Google Play Billing and related components. The presence of a network permission does not mean the developer receives your reading history. Processing by Google Play's distribution, security and system services is described in the next section.

5. Google Play, asset delivery and purchases

When you use Google Play to download, install or update the App and its painting asset pack, or use its licensing and installation-security checks, Google may process Google Account, app-version, device and installation information relevant to those services. This processing supports distribution, compatibility, security and related platform features and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and your Google settings. It does not mean that the developer receives all information held by the platform.

Where applicable conditions are met, such as your allowing the device to share usage and diagnostics data, Google Play may also make aggregate statistics and technical diagnostics available to the developer through platform features such as Android vitals. These may include app version, device model, Android version, crash reports and application-not-responding reports. We use available reports only to investigate problems and improve stability and compatibility, not to create advertising profiles. This differs from the App uploading the specific dates or historical entries you read. Android and Google services manage the relevant sharing settings.

The App includes the Google Play Billing component, but real purchases are not enabled in the current internal-testing release. If an interface says “Purchases are not available yet”, that entry point will not start a real transaction. The App does not currently send orders or purchase tokens to the developer through that flow. We do not ask for your bank-card number, payment password or full payment-card details through the App.

Before enabling real purchases, purchase restoration or receipt verification, we will update this policy to describe the actual handling of information such as purchase status, product identifiers or purchase tokens. Account, transaction and download records already held by Google Play remain subject to Google's management and policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en
