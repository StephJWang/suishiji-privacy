1. Scope and current release

This policy describes the App's current features and distinguishes on-device processing, information actually received by the developer, and processing by service providers such as Google and GitHub. A Google Play account is not a Suishiji account. The App currently has no account registration, cloud-based personal profile, or developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

The App supports a one-time Full Reading purchase through Google Play to unlock all entries in historical events on this day and notable births and deaths. No Suishiji account is required; purchases and purchase restoration use Google Play's account and billing services.

2. Information processed on your device

Dates and interface state. The App uses the local date and time supplied by your device to display “today”. It also processes your selected date, lunar or Gregorian mode, and daily or monthly view while you use it. These values support display and calculations; they are not sent to the developer as search or reading history. The App does not read private events from your system calendar or ask for your own birthday.

Text preferences. Your choice of Simplified or Traditional Chinese is stored in the App's local settings so it can be restored the next time you open the App. This setting does not contain your name, email address or other account information.

Content and caches. Historical text, calendar data, fonts and paintings are provided through the App and its asset packs. Loading and displaying this content may use temporary memory or system-managed caches on your device. Core reading does not require uploading the dates, text or paintings you view to the developer.

Purchase records. The App saves purchase data returned by Google Play and verified locally, together with its signature, in private on-device storage. This includes the order ID, app and product identifiers, purchase time, purchase status and purchase token, and is used to verify Full Reading access and retain offline reading. The App does not upload this local record to a developer-operated server. Interactions with Google Play are described in section 5.

Review access. If a review access code is used, the App checks it locally and saves only an authorization marker (a digest of the code). It does not store the entered plaintext code or upload it. This authorization is separate from real purchase records and creates no order or charge.

3. Android backups and device transfers

The App currently permits Android system backups. Depending on your device, operating-system version, backup provider and personal settings, the system may back up eligible App data, such as local settings, to your backup account, or transfer that data when you change devices. On devices using Google backup, this service is generally provided by Google. The App excludes local purchase records and review authorizations from system backups and device transfers. After changing devices or reinstalling, purchased access is restored by querying Google Play with the same account.

A system backup does not mean that the developer receives the data. We cannot read your system backups through our own server. You can manage backup, restoration and deletion through your device settings and backup service. Uninstalling the App or clearing its local data does not necessarily delete existing cloud backups.

4. Permissions, advertising and tracking

The current App does not request access to your location, contacts, SMS, call history, camera, microphone or personal photo library, and does not need access to your private calendar events. It does not display advertising or integrate advertising SDKs, behavioral-analytics SDKs, or an additional crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal information or create advertising profiles.
